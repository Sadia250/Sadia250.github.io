How I test a login page

Test scenarios

After SQA training in manual and automation testing, this is the checklist I start with for a login form. Each row is one scenario, with the input I try and what I expect to see.

ScenarioInputExpected resultOutcome
Valid loginRegistered email and correct passwordUser signs in and lands on the right pageAccepted
Wrong passwordRegistered email, incorrect passwordClear error message, no sign-inRejected
Unknown userEmail that is not registeredError message, no account details revealedRejected
Empty fieldsBoth fields blank, then one blankValidation message on the missing fieldRejected
BoundariesShortest and longest length, spaces, special characters, bad email formatHandled without errors or a crashRejected
InjectionSQL injection text in each fieldInput rejected, no data exposedRejected
Repeated failuresSeveral wrong attempts in a rowLockout or delay after the limitRejected
Session endLog out, then press the browser Back buttonProtected pages stay hiddenAccepted
